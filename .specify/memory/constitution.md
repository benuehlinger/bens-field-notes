# Ben's Field Notes — current principles

Version 2.0.0 · Amended 25 November 2026 at Ben's request to pivot the finance journal into an AI engineering knowledge space.

## Purpose

Help Ben learn, keep up and get involved. Questions, evidence, experiments and useful connections take priority over collecting more bookmarks. The source repository provides working context for agents. Earlier finance specs describe a historical site.

## Publishing

Use Quarto pages and, when requested, Jupyter notebooks. Keep the site static and maintainable. Sources and topic relationships live in portable JSON. New authoring can grow without a framework rewrite or parallel database.

## Evidence

Cite factual claims and date changing information. Separate source claims, interpretation, proposed experiments and observed results. Figures identify data, scope, units and transformations. Contribution guides establish a route; an open opportunity needs its own verification.

## Inquiry

Follow Ben's questions one notebook block at a time. Read `docs/NOTEBOOK-WORKFLOW.md` before starting a notebook. Preserve failed experiments and revisions when they explain how understanding changed.

## Design

The current direction is cutting edge and minimal, using Vercel as a visual reference. Use dark neutral surfaces, clear sans typography, subtle rules and purposeful data visualization. Support light mode, keyboard access, small screens and reduced motion. Keep the voice personal and curious.

## Scope

Finance content is retired from the active site and recoverable in Git. Preserve unrelated uncommitted work. Use README.md and AGENTS.md as entry points; historical specs and finance article skills do not prescribe the new knowledge pages.
